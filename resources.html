Start From Zero

Never played, or played once a decade ago. Any one of these will get you to a full game.

Lichess Learn

Interactive lessons, free, no account needed. The best place to start.

Chess.com Lessons

Guided interactive course. Paid, but well structured.

GothamChess — Ultimate Beginner Guide

The rules and the first ideas, in one video.

ChessNetwork — How to Play Chess

The same ground, slower, from a National Master.

Get Better

For when you know the rules and keep losing anyway.

Chessbrah — Building Habits

A Grandmaster climbing from a low rating, explaining every move.

Chessable

Spaced-repetition training for openings, tactics, and endgames.

Aimchess

Reads your own games and tells you what you keep getting wrong.
